## Design decisions worth defending

* **Frozen, committed corpus; generator as reproducibility tooling.** Delta-detection eval
  needs fixed ground truth; regeneration drift would silently invalidate the ledger. The
  case-log generator is fully deterministic (no RNG, index-derived values) and a test
  asserts committed bytes == regenerated bytes.
* **Ground truth in a sidecar, never in the data.** `cases.jsonl` contains no delta
  vocabulary at all (tested with a leak-check); labels live in `ground_truth_tags.json`.
  Otherwise phase-3 pattern mining grades itself on leaked answers — interview Q9 territory.
* **Error cases are near-delta on purpose.** E5 skips the callback on a $50k account
  (outside the tacit under-$10k threshold), E6 approves through a two-mismatch address —
  both are the confusions a lazy detector reports as pattern support. They're labeled
  `error`, never delta-tagged, and count against precision if detected.
* **D4/D5 traces also appear in policy-consistent cases** — screening-first ordering and
  60-day strictness don't *violate* written policy, so compliant files can still carry
  the signal. The detector should find them from sequence/notes evidence, not from the
  case being "in the tacit bucket."
* **Fictional high-risk jurisdictions** (Kavastan, Zubaria, Port Meridian) — no coupling
  to live FATF list churn, no real-country mislabeling in a public repo.
* **Red herrings in every transcript** (portal timeouts, staffing, dashboards) so
  extraction has something to correctly ignore.

## Things Adi must be able to explain cold

1. Why does authored synthetic data not invalidate the delta-detection eval, and what
   WOULD? (See SYNTHETIC.md — measurement of pipeline recovery vs organic-speech claim;
   invalidated by label leakage, ledger-paraphrase voicing, or tuning against the ledger.)
2. Why is D10 special? (Conflict *between practitioners* — the graph must represent
   intra-practice disagreement, not just policy-vs-practice; two customers with the same
   facts got opposite outcomes.)
3. Why can a policy-consistent case carry a D4 tag? (Stricter/re-sequenced ≠ non-compliant;
   the delta is divergence-from-written, not a violation.)
4. Walk through how a single tacit case supports three deltas at once (e.g. HC-051:
   two address mismatches + widened match tolerance + skipped callback — one applicant,
   three floor practices visible in one record).
5. Why hand-assigned delta distributions instead of random sampling? (Support counts are
   ledger-exact and auditable; randomness buys nothing but flaky evals.)
